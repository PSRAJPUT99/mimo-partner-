# Mimo Partner

## Vision
AI Employee for Every Small Business.

## Problem
Small businesses don't have time to answer customers, manage leads, and automate repetitive work.

## Solution
An AI assistant that can chat with customers, answer questions, capture leads, and automate business tasks.

## MVP Features
- AI Chat
- Knowledge Base
- Lead Capture
- Dashboard
- Authentication

## Target Users
- Small Businesses
- Freelancers
- Agencies
- Coaches
